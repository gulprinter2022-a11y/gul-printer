Who We Are

More than a print shop

Gul Printer combines graphic design knowledge with hands-on printing experience. This allows us to think about a project from both sides: how it should look on screen and how it will perform in final print.

Our focus is simple: clear communication, careful design, suitable print materials and reliable service.

Our Journey

Three stages that shaped Gul Printer

1

Gul Online Designer

The journey began from a small room at home with a single computer and a focus on digital design. Alongside this, remote work with a freelancing company introduced international clients, overseas projects and wider design standards.

2

Gul Designer & Printer

The next stage was practical work inside a well-known printing press, learning modern printing technology, machinery, paper quality and production requirements while handling NGO, educational, madrasa and corporate work.

3

Gul Printer Peshawar

After leaving the job, an independent setup was launched in the press market area of Mohalla Jangi, Peshawar. Loyal early customers and referrals helped the business grow into an online and offline service provider for customers across Pakistan.
