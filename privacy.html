Privacy Policy

This page explains how Gul Printer handles information shared through this website, WhatsApp, phone, email and social media contact.

Effective date: October 2026. This policy applies to the Gul Printer website and direct customer enquiries.

1. Information We May Receive

Gul Printer does not currently use customer accounts, online payment forms or an online order form on this website. However, if you contact us directly, you may choose to provide information such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, business or institution name, project details, quantities, files or artwork.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To discuss design, printing and production requirements.
  • To prepare, manage and deliver customer projects.
  • To provide customer support and follow-up.

3. WhatsApp, Phone, Email and Social Media

When you use WhatsApp, phone, email, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Google Maps links, your interaction may also be governed by the privacy practices of those third-party services. Gul Printer does not control how those services process information.

4. Files and Artwork

Files, artwork, logos, documents and other materials sent by customers are used for the requested design or printing work. Customers should avoid sending unnecessary confidential information inside project files.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer information. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to complete a requested service, for example with a production, printing, delivery or technical service provider, or when required by law.

6. Website Hosting and Technical Data

This website is hosted using third-party web infrastructure. Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type or access logs may be processed by the hosting provider for security, performance and reliability purposes.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Gul Printer does not currently operate a custom analytics or advertising tracking system on this website. If analytics, advertising or additional cookies are added later, this policy will be updated where appropriate.

8. Data Retention

Customer communication and project information may be retained for as long as reasonably useful for project completion, record keeping, repeat work, customer support or legal requirements.

9. Your Choices

You may contact Gul Printer if you want to ask what personal information you have directly shared with us, request a correction, or ask us to delete information where there is no legal or operational reason to retain it.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, business processes or legal requirements change. The latest version published on this website will apply.
